The Word, The Name, The Meaning

In the Hebrew language these four letters became the Tetragrammaton, YHWH, the Lord (Ba'al) of the North, or (Ba'al) of Lebanon, also called (Ba'al) Tsaphon, this name can be interpreted as (Lord of the North). It was never used as a name proper, (Ba'al) was only used to hide the true name of God. (Ba'al) became as popular as the images of Jupiter.

Y.H.W.H. (Yahweh) comprised of four letters, which mean “that which was, that which is, that which shall be.”

The name of God of the Hebrews really is complete in only three letters, the four letter is a copy of the second letter.

The Jews say that the right pronunciation of the name of God does exist on Earth and it's one of the mysteries that the Messiah will reveal.
