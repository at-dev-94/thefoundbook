Phoenician Tsaphon

The Babylonian God Enki was the creator God Yahweh-Jehovah, first named in the Phoenician myths as Ba'al Saphon, the Thunder and Lightening God of the Hebrews, also known as Ba'al Tsaphon.

Enki had helped man before the flood, fulfilled the function of the Hebrew Creator God Yahweh-Jehovah of the Old Testament. Enki was the original amphibious god.

Encylopdia of World Mythology. Foreward by Rex Warner. BPC. Pub. 1975. Page 119.

Ba'al Saphon, first found in Palestine and Southern Syria, named Adad in the Mesopotamian texts and Hadad in texts from Ras Shamra in Northern Syria.

Flying Serpents and Dragons. R.A. Boulay. Galaxy Books. Florida 1991. Page 37.

Adad was in all probability the Yahweh Jehovah of the Old Testament.

New Larousse Encylopdia of Mythology. Hamlyn London 1959. Page 73.

Ba'al, in the Ras Shamrah texts, is the same god as Ba'al Tsaphon.

Sirius Mystery. R.K.G. Temple. Destiny Books. Vermont 1976. Page 125.

It was, in fact, Enki who assisted man before the flood came by warning the proto-Noah of the Sumerian deluge story to build his ark. He thus fulfilled the function of the special presiding deity of the Hebrews, the Jehovah of the Old Testament. How many Jews know that their god was originally amphibious?
