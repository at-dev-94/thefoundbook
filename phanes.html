Grecian Phanes

Phanes is called the Intelligible Light, as well as the Limit or Boundry, Phanes was also called Eros or Love, it is the Light or Boundry because Phanes is visible and seeable. The Revealed One, the Word, the Logos.

Phaen(o) — Shining, show, appear.

(PHANES) “LIGHT” was the very first thing to appear. It is said by Orpheus, the Father of all the Gods was PHANES.

Dictionary all Scriptures and Myths. G.A. Gaskell. Gramercy Books 1960. Page 243 & 571.

The first to appear is “LIGHT” (PHANES). Orpheus tells us that PHANES is the Father of all the Gods. Phanes was also called (Eros) … also the Limit or Boundary, also as Intelligible Light, and the Light of Truth.

Isis Unveiled. Blavatsky. Theosophical University Press 1976. Page 146–7.

It was the God Phanes, the Revealed One, the Word, or Logos.

Shorter Oxford Dictionary of Historical Principles. Claredon Press 1973. Page 1568.

Phen-Pheno-Formitive element in chemistry, (for phaen(o) shining, show, appear).
